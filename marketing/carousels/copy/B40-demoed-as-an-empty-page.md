## Post
I went to show somebody the shipping side and it opened on nothing.

The feature worked. It had shipped, it had tests, and the week before it had priced fifty shipments across five supplier warehouses and ten offices in 254 seconds.

But the tenant I demo from is deliberately empty, because I will not show anybody a real book. So what the other person saw was a heading with white space under it.

Nothing was broken, and that is what made it worse.

A blank screen does not read as no data. It reads as unfinished. The viewer cannot tell the difference and they will not ask you to clarify. They just quietly downgrade everything else you showed them.

So an empty state owes the viewer four things. Say what lives here. Say why it is empty right now. Show the shape with one sample row, clearly labelled as a sample. Give the single action that fills it. Never leave a bare heading over white space.

Same code, same day, completely different conversation. One version ends the topic. The other gets you the question you actually wanted, which is how does the data get in.

Every business has a version of this. The catalogue with photos on half the items. The quote with a blank lead time. The price list where four rows say call us. The reader does not see missing information. They see a company that did not finish.

Go look at the thing you send out most often and find the blank.
